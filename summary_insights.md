# Executive Summary Insights

- Biggest companies by total revenue (absolute): Amazon, Apple, Google, Microsoft, Samsung.
- Highest operating margins (among scoped firms): Nvidia, Microsoft, TSMC, Texas Instruments, Apple.
- Largest TAM activities: AI/ML Accelerators, Foundry Services, Client Computing/PCs, Memory.
- Fastest-growing activities: AI/ML Accelerators, Data Center GPUs, Edge AI, Autonomous Vehicles & ADAS.
- Most diversified across listed activities: Intel, Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, Samsung.
- Intel strength areas: Client CPUs, Data Center CPUs, FPGAs, Foundry (emerging), networking.
- Intel weaker/absent areas: Memory (no direct merchant DRAM/NAND), EDA software, limited automotive infotainment scale.
- Interpretation caveats: activity-level values are estimated and non-additive across overlapping categories; hyperscaler silicon economics are partially internal transfer-value approximations.
